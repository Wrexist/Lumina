Overview

Lumina is an astrology app. To generate a real birth chart we need your birth date, time, and place; to write a grounded reading we process the astrological facts computed from that chart; to run a subscription we work with Apple's payment system. This policy explains what that involves in plain terms.

What never leaves your device

Palm reading is not part of the current release. When it ships it will be captured and analyzed entirely on your iPhone, and the photo will never be uploaded — but until it does, Lumina neither requests camera access nor processes any palm data.

Your journal entries and cached chart data are also stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework, protected behind Face ID if you enable app lock in Settings.

Third-party services

Lumina is built on a small number of specialized services, each used for exactly one purpose:

  • Our own ephemeris service — a backend we operate to compute real planetary positions from your birth data. This is not a third party; it's infrastructure we run specifically for chart accuracy.
  • Anthropic (Claude) — generates the written text of your interpretations. It receives the astrological facts already computed from your chart (for example, "Sun in Gemini, Moon in Pisces, Saturn retrograde") — never your name, contact details, or account information.
  • RevenueCat — manages subscription entitlements. All payment itself is processed by Apple; we never see or store your card details.
  • Supabase — used for account sync and push-token storage where account features are enabled.
  • OneSignal — delivers push notifications, only if you turn them on. Disabling notifications in Settings stops this entirely.
  • Apple — App Store purchases, Face ID, and camera access are all mediated by iOS itself under Apple's own privacy protections.

We do not use advertising networks, ad-tracking SDKs, or sell any personal data to third parties.

Storage & security

Data that does leave your device (birth data sent for chart computation, chart facts sent for interpretation) travels over encrypted connections. Where account data is stored server-side, it's protected behind authenticated access — there is no public database of user charts.

Your rights

Open Settings → Account → Delete account in the app to delete your account and everything associated with it. That removes your server-side account, revokes the Sign in with Apple credential, and erases your birth data, journal entries, friends, and chart history from this device. Uninstalling the app also removes everything stored on it.

Open Settings → Privacy → Export my data in the app to write everything Lumina holds — your birth data, journal entries, saved people and settings — to a single JSON file you can save or send anywhere. It runs on the device, needs no request, and no one has to approve it.

You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime from your Apple ID settings — see Support for the exact steps. You can disable notifications, camera access, or Face ID individually in the iOS Settings app at any time without losing access to the rest of Lumina.

Children's privacy

Lumina is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we'll remove it.

This website

This marketing website (the pages you're reading right now) does not use tracking cookies, third-party analytics, or advertising pixels of any kind. It's static, informational pages — nothing about your visit here is collected.

Changes to this policy

If Lumina's data practices change in a way that affects you, we'll update this page and, for material changes, notify you in the app before the change takes effect.
