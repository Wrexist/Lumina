Support

Questions, answered plainly.

No runaround. If something's unclear about how Lumina works, it's probably here.

Do I need to enter my exact birth time?

Not to get started — your Sun, Moon, and planetary signs are accurate from your birth date and place alone. Your birth time is what unlocks your Ascendant (rising sign), Midheaven, and house placements, since those depend on precisely where the sky was relative to the horizon at your exact moment of birth.

If you don't know it, Lumina tells you plainly which parts of your chart are hidden rather than guessing — look for the "Houses are hidden" note on your Chart tab. You can add a birth time later in Settings → Your info at any time.

Does Lumina do palm reading?

Not in the current release. We're building on-device line tracing and won't ship it until it works fairly across every skin tone. When it does arrive, the photo will be analyzed on your phone and never uploaded. Today the app doesn't request camera access at all.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

All Lumina subscriptions are billed through your Apple ID, so they're managed the same way as any App Store subscription: open the Settings app on your iPhone → tap your name at the top → Subscriptions → Lumina. From there you can switch plans, turn off auto-renew, or cancel — it takes effect at the end of your current billing period.

Lumina offers monthly and annual plans only. We don't run weekly subscriptions or any billing outside Apple's system.

What house system does Lumina use?

Placidus by default, with Whole-Sign and Sidereal available as alternatives — switch anytime from the house-system picker on your Chart tab. Whichever you choose, the underlying planetary positions are identical; only the house division changes.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. Settings → Account → Delete account removes your account and erases everything on the device. For a copy of your data, email privacy@lumina.app — a self-serve export is planned but not available yet. See our Privacy Policy for the full detail on what's stored and where.

Is the daily reading personalized, or the same for everyone with my sign?

Personalized. Your daily reading is generated from the real transits happening today against your specific natal chart — not a generic, sign-wide horoscope. Two people with the same Sun sign but different birth times and places will get different readings, because their charts are different.

Does Lumina support Human Design?

Yes — switch to Human Design mode on the Chart tab for your bodygraph, defined centers, and gate activations, computed from the same underlying chart data.

Why is this different from other astrology apps?

Most of the category does at least one of three things: approximates planetary positions instead of computing them properly, fakes palm reading with a stock animation, or uses billing patterns designed to make canceling hard. Lumina computes real positions from a genuine ephemeris, doesn't ship palm reading until it actually works, and bills only through transparent monthly or annual Apple subscriptions.
