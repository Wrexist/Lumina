# Screenshots and app preview — the storyboard

Screenshots are the highest-leverage conversion surface on the product page:
most people decide from the first two without ever expanding the description.
Since June 2025 the caption text is also OCR'd into a weak ranking signal, so
every caption below is written to carry a real keyword as well as a real
promise.

## Required sizes

| Display | Pixels (portrait) | Required |
|---|---|---|
| 6.9" (iPhone 16 Pro Max / 17 Pro Max) | 1320 × 2868 | **Yes** — Apple scales this set down for every smaller iPhone |
| 6.5" (iPhone 11 Pro Max / XS Max) | 1242 × 2688 | Only if you want a distinct set for older devices |
| iPad 13" | 2064 × 2752 | Only if the app ships for iPad — 1.0 is `TARGETED_DEVICE_FAMILY: "1"`, iPhone only, so **skip** |

Ship **one** 6.9" set of 6 shots. Uploading a single required set and letting
Apple scale is standard and saves a full round of production per release.

**Five of the six are generated by CI.** `AppStoreShotTests` renders them at
440 × 956 points at scale 3 — exactly 1320 × 2868 pixels — on every run, and
the `app-store-screenshots` artifact on the iOS job is the download. A test in
the same file fails the build if any of them isn't the exact size App Store
Connect requires, so a rejected upload can't be the way you find out.

The one exception is noted per-frame below: the 3D moon is a SceneKit
`UIViewRepresentable`, which `ImageRenderer` cannot drive. Capture it by hand,
or ship the five — five honest screenshots beat six with one blank frame.

For anything captured by hand, use the iPhone 16 Pro Max simulator with the
demo birth data from the review notes (14 March 1990, 09:25, Stockholm) so
every screen is populated — an empty state in a screenshot reads as a broken
app.

## The six

Order matters: 1–3 are what the store shows in search results, and they carry
the argument on their own.

**1 — Today, revealed** · generated (`01-today.png`)
> Caption: **Your daily horoscope, from real transits**
> Shows: the reading card with the transit sentences visible, moon phase card
> beneath. This is the app's core loop and the highest-volume search intent
> ("daily horoscope") in one frame.

**2 — Why these?** · capture by hand
> Caption: **Tap any line. See the exact chart data behind it.**
> Shows: the transparency sheet — planet marks, "transiting Mars trine natal
> Venus · 1.2° orb · applying", and the Earth vantage note. Nothing else in
> the category shows its work; lead with it while it is still rare.

**3 — The chart wheel** · generated (`02-birth-chart.png`)
> Caption: **Your birth chart, computed — not guessed**
> Shows: the interactive wheel with the Big Three band. Carries `birth chart`,
> the term the app name bets on.

**4 — The moon** · capture by hand (SceneKit)
> Caption: **Tonight's real moon phase, in 3D**
> Shows: `MoonSphereSheetView` with the new lunar texture at a gibbous phase.
> The single most visually striking screen in the app — and a real feature,
> lit by the true Sun–Moon–Earth angle.

**5 — People / synastry** · generated (`03-synastry.png`)
> Reflect is generated too (`05-reflect.png`) — a sixth frame if you want it.
> Caption: **Synastry and composite charts for the people you add**
> Shows: the People list with constellation avatars, one friend detail with
> real aspects. Carries `synastry` + `composite`.

**6 — Human Design** · generated (`04-human-design.png`)
> Caption: **Human Design, from the same birth data**
> Shows: the bodygraph with defined centres. Adjacent category, high intent,
> and it is what Plus mostly sells.
>
> Rendered from `TabPreviews.chartWithDefinedChannels()`, not the shared
> sample chart. The shared one completes no channel, so the frame came out as
> nine empty boxes — a correct render (Lumina computes 11 of the 26
> activations a full bodygraph uses) and a useless screenshot.
> `testTheBodygraphFrameHasChannelsToShow` fails the build if that regresses.
> The caption lost the word "bodygraph" because the longer line wrapped to
> three, pushing the frame's crop line up.

### Caption rules

- Under 45 characters where possible — longer runs get truncated on the
  smallest devices and read as noise in search results.
- One idea per caption, in sentence case, no emoji (brand rule).
- Do not caption a screen with a promise the screen doesn't show.
- Never caption a feature the build lacks. `scripts/aso_lint.py` guards the
  text fields; captions are images, so this one is on you.

## App preview video (optional for 1.0)

15–30 seconds, captured on device, no voiceover, no stock music beds with
licensing questions attached. Apple autoplays it muted, so it has to work
silently.

Storyboard:

| Seconds | Shot |
|---|---|
| 0–4 | Today opens, veil card taps, reading unveils |
| 4–9 | Scroll to the moon card, tap it, the 3D moon rotates |
| 9–15 | Chart tab: wheel draws, tap a planet, detail sheet with the sphere hero |
| 15–22 | "Why these?" sheet — the raw transit data, held long enough to read one line |
| 22–28 | People: add a friend, compatibility resolves to a real score |

Skip the preview entirely for 1.0 if it is the last thing standing between the
build and submission. Six good screenshots outperform a rushed video.

## Production notes

- Use the real app, not a mockup. Apple rejects screenshots that show a UI the
  app doesn't have.
- The status bar should read a clean time and full battery — `xcrun simctl
  status_bar <device> override --time "9:41" --batteryLevel 100`.
- No device frames drawn *inside* the image unless the whole set uses them
  consistently.
- Keep captions in the top third: the bottom is where Apple overlays its own
  chrome in some placements.
